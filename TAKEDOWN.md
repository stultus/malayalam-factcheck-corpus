# Takedown policy

If you are a rights holder, source publisher, or named individual and you would like content removed from this corpus or from any derived release, contact:

**hello@stultus.in**

Please include:

- The URL(s) of the article(s) in question, **or** the `record_id` if you have it from a published corpus release.
- Your relationship to the content (publisher, author, named individual, legal representative).
- The action you are requesting (full removal, removal of a specific field such as `evidence_text`, anonymisation, attribution change).

## Response commitments

- Acknowledgement within **5 working days**.
- Action within **15 working days** of acknowledgement, barring genuine ambiguity in the request.
- For any released corpus version, removed records will be stripped from the next published version. The previous version will be marked deprecated in its release notes; we cannot recall copies that have already been downloaded.
- For records still only in the local pipeline (not yet released), removal is immediate and the URL is added to a denylist so the pipeline will not re-collect it.

## Disputed claims

If a publisher disputes that a particular debunked claim was correctly attributed to them, we will remove the record pending verification rather than litigate the attribution.

## Out of scope

This policy is for content originally published by Indian fact-check sources and ingested by this pipeline. It is not a forum for re-litigating the underlying fact-check verdicts themselves — for that, contact the original publisher.
